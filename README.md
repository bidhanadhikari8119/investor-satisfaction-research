# Investor Satisfaction through Service Quality: Evidence from Pokhara Brokerage Firms

**Peer-reviewed research article** · *The Journal of Nepalese Business Studies*, Vol. 17, No. 1, December 2024, pp. 59–69

**DOI:** [10.3126/jnbs.v17i1.75257](https://doi.org/10.3126/jnbs.v17i1.75257) · **Full paper:** [PDF](Adhikari_Adhikari_2024_Investor_Satisfaction_JNBS.pdf)

**Authors:** Nabin Bahadur Adhikari ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0005-1103-6855)) and **Bidhan Adhikari** ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-0773-7476))

---

## The question
Brokerage firms connect investors to Nepal's stock market. **Which parts of a broker's service actually make investors satisfied?** The study tested three service quality factors:
- **Reliability:** doing the service right the first time, on time, with no errors
- **Responsiveness:** staff being quick and willing to help
- **Assurance:** knowledgeable staff, and investors feeling safe in their transactions

## Data and method
- **Survey of 105 stock market investors** in Pokhara, Nepal (5-point Likert scale questionnaire)
- **Reliability check:** Cronbach's alpha above 0.6 for every scale
- **Correlation analysis** between each service factor and investor satisfaction
- **Multiple linear regression** in SPSS to measure each factor's impact on satisfaction

## Key findings
1. **Assurance matters most.** It had the strongest link to satisfaction (r = 0.72) and the biggest impact: each 1-point rise in assurance raised satisfaction by 0.43 points (p < 0.001).
2. **Reliability also matters.** Each 1-point rise raised satisfaction by 0.28 points (p = 0.005).
3. **Responsiveness did not have a significant impact** once the other factors were included (p = 0.32), even though it was correlated with satisfaction on its own (r = 0.60).
4. **Together, the three factors explain 44.7% of the differences in investor satisfaction** (R² = 0.447, F = 27.26).

## What it means for business
Brokerage firms that want satisfied investors should invest first in **staff knowledge and secure transactions (assurance)** and in **error-free, on-time service (reliability)**. Faster responses alone are not enough.
